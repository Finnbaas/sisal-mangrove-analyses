# Mangrove maps

Maps of mangrove for your own study area, made in your own Google Earth Engine account. They use the same method as the Vegetation page of the [Sisal Mangrove Monitor](https://github.com/Finnbaas/sisal-mangrove-monitor). Each map is a standalone package: everything you need is in its folder.

| Map | What it shows | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [change](change/) | Where mangrove disappeared or appeared between 1993 and 2025: stable, confirmed loss, confirmed gain, uncertain change, with areas per class. | Available (beta) |
| Condition | How healthy the mangrove is. | Coming soon |
| Disturbance and recovery | Where mangrove was disturbed and whether it recovered. | Coming soon |
