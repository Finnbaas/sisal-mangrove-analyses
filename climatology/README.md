# Climatology analyses

- [event-anomaly](event-anomaly/): anomaly chart around an event for your own data.
- [seasonal-cycle](seasonal-cycle/): monthly mean with percentile band (a typical year).
- [long-term-trend](long-term-trend/): annual means and trend with significance.
- [monthly-range](monthly-range/): monthly lowest-to-highest band and 365-day running mean.
- [wave-wind-rose](wave-wind-rose/): where waves or wind come from, all days vs rough days.
- [event-counts](event-counts/): events per month and per year, with a HURDAT2 storm list helper.
- [storm-tracks](storm-tracks/): map of storm tracks near a location, coloured by strength.
